Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds are assessed on a case-by-case basis, reflecting the bespoke nature of our creative services. Eligibility primarily hinges on the phase of the project and the deliverables provided to date.

  • check_circle Pre-Commencement: If a cancellation is requested before any formal creative work or strategy has begun, a full refund (minus any transaction fees) may be issued.
  • check_circle Discovery Phase: Should you choose to terminate the agreement during the initial strategy or discovery phase, a partial refund will be provided, retaining a pro-rated amount for hours invested.
