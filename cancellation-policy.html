Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans change. Our cancellation and rescheduling policies are designed to be fair to both our clients and our creative professionals whose time is secured for your project. Please review the terms below carefully.

Last Updated: October 15, 2024

event_busy

Standard Cancellations

More than 72 hours notice

Cancellations made more than 72 hours before the scheduled project start time are eligible for a full refund of the deposit, minus a 5% administrative processing fee.

Between 24 and 72 hours notice

Cancellations made within this window will forfeit the 50% non-refundable deposit required at booking. Any additional payments made will be refunded.

Less than 24 hours notice

Cancellations made within 24 hours of the scheduled start time, or 'no-shows', will result in a charge for the full remaining balance of the project invoice.

update

Rescheduling

We offer one complimentary reschedule per project if requested at least 48 hours in advance, subject to availability.

  • check_circle Subsequent reschedules incur a 15% rescheduling fee based on the total project cost.
  • check_circle Reschedule requests within 24 hours are treated as cancellations.
cloud_off

Force Majeure & Inclement Weather

For on-location shoots highly dependent on weather conditions, the creative team will make a call 24 hours prior. If a shoot must be postponed due to severe weather, the client will be offered alternative dates without penalty.

In the event of an act of God, major illness, or other unavoidable circumstances that prevent SL Creative from executing the project, all deposits will be refunded in full, or the project will be rescheduled at the client's discretion.

Need to make a change?

If you need to cancel or reschedule your upcoming session, please contact your dedicated producer immediately.
